# RETAIL UNSECURED LENDING CREDIT POLICY (SOUTH AFRICA)

**Document Classification:** INTERNAL CONFIDENTIAL  
**Document Owner:** Chief Risk Officer (CRO)  
**Effective Date:** January 2026  
**Next Review Date:** January 2027  
**Applicability:** All Retail Unsecured Credit Products (Personal Loans, Revolving Credit Facilities, Credit Cards)  

---

## 1. REGULATORY & LEGAL COMPLIANCE
All credit origination, assessment, and lifecycle management activities must strictly adhere to the South African regulatory framework:
* **National Credit Act (NCA) 34 of 2005:** No credit may be extended without a comprehensive, documented affordability assessment to prevent reckless lending.
* **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) 4 of 2013:** Customer data must be securely processed, stored, and explicitly consented to for credit bureau inquiries.
* **Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) 38 of 2001:** Mandates strict KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) verification prior to onboarding.

---

## 2. MINIMUM QUALIFICATION (KNOCK-OUT) CRITERIA
An application is subject to an **Immediate Auto-Decline** if *any* of the following conditions are met:
* **Age:** Applicant is under 18 or over 65 years of age at the time of application.
* **Residency:** Applicant does not possess a valid South African green barcoded ID book or Smart ID card. (Foreign nationals require a valid work permit and a manual underwriter review).
* **Minimum Income:** Verifiable Gross Monthly Income is less than R 5,000.
* **Employment Status:** Applicant is unemployed, under notice of retrenchment, or in probation (less than 3 months with current employer).
* **Bureau Legal Status:** Applicant is currently under Debt Review, Administration, Sequestration, or has an active court judgment against them.
* **Bureau Default Status:** Applicant currently holds a 90+ days in arrears status (Default) on *any* active tradeline across TransUnion, Experian, or XDS.
* **Fraud:** Applicant is listed on the South African Fraud Prevention Service (SAFPS) database.

---

## 3. AFFORDABILITY ASSESSMENT RULES
In accordance with the NCA, a rigorous affordability calculation must be executed to ensure the consumer has sufficient discretionary income.

### 3.1. Income & Expense Calculations
* **Net Income Calculation:** Gross Income minus statutory deductions (PAYE tax, UIF) and mandatory deductions (maintenance orders, union fees).
* **Living Expenses:** Must be calculated using the *higher* of:
    1. The customer's self-declared minimum living expenses.
    2. The Minimum Expense Norms table published annually by the National Credit Regulator (NCR).

### 3.2. Ratio Limits
* **Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio Cap: 45%** *Calculation: (Total Monthly Debt Obligations from Bureau + Proposed Loan Installment) / Gross Monthly Income.*
* **Payment-to-Income (PTI) Ratio Cap: 30%** *Calculation: Proposed Loan Installment / Net Monthly Income.*
* **Interest Rate Buffer:** For variable-rate loans, a mandatory 200 basis points (2.00%) interest rate shock must be applied to the proposed installment during the affordability check to ensure future affordability.

---

## 4. RISK-BASED STRATEGY & SCORECARD CUT-OFFS
The core decision engine utilizes a Logistic Regression Scorecard. The resultant Probability of Default (PD) and corresponding Credit Score (Range: 300 - 850) dictate the applicant's Risk Band.

### 🔴 HIGH RISK BAND (PD > 20.00% | Score < 600)
* **Decision:** Auto-Decline.
* **Rationale:** Exceeds the bank's risk appetite for unsecured exposure.
* **Exceptions:** See Section 7 (Delegated Authority).

### 🟡 MEDIUM RISK BAND (PD 10.01% - 20.00% | Score 600 - 679)
* **Decision:** Refer to Underwriter / Manual Review.
* **Maximum Unsecured Exposure:** R 150,000.
* **Maximum Term:** 48 Months.
* **Pricing:** Prime + 8.00% to Prime + 14.00% (Subject to NCA maximums).
* **Verification Requirements:** Requires 3 months of stamped, consecutive bank statements and manual income verification via recent employer payslips.

### 🟢 LOW RISK BAND (PD 0.00% - 10.00% | Score 680 - 850)
* **Decision:** Auto-Approve (Straight-Through Processing).
* **Maximum Unsecured Exposure:** R 350,000.
* **Maximum Term:** 72 Months.
* **Pricing:** Prime + 2.00% to Prime + 7.99% (Discounted rates applied to highest scores).
* **Verification Requirements:** Automated income estimation via bureau account turnover is acceptable.

---

## 5. NCA FEE LIMITATIONS
All approved credit agreements must strictly adhere to the pricing caps set by the National Credit Regulator:
* **Initiation Fee:** Capped at R 165 per credit agreement, plus 10% of the amount of the principal debt in excess of R 1,000. The absolute maximum Initiation Fee may never exceed R 1,050 (excluding VAT).
* **Monthly Service Fee:** Capped at a maximum of R 60 per month (excluding VAT).
* **Maximum Interest Rate:** The repo rate (SA Reserve Bank) multiplied by 2.2, plus 20% per year.

---

## 6. BEHAVIORAL & BUREAU POLICY RULES
Even if an applicant passes the primary scorecard, specific behavioral triggers will alter the final decision.

* **Recent Inquiries:** More than 4 unsecured credit inquiries in the last 6 months across the banking sector indicates credit distress. **Rule:** Downgrade applicant by one Risk Band (e.g., Low becomes Medium). If already Medium, trigger Auto-Decline.
* **Revolving Utilization:** If the applicant's aggregate revolving credit utilization (Credit Cards, Overdrafts, Store Cards) exceeds 85% of their total limits, the maximum approved loan amount must be reduced by 25%.
* **Early Delinquency:** Any 30-day or 60-day arrears occurring within the last 3 months on an existing tradeline will trigger a mandatory Underwriter Review, regardless of the applicant's PD.

---

## 7. DELEGATED AUTHORITY & OVERRIDES (EXCEPTIONS)
Automated decisions may only be overridden according to strict internal mandates. Override rates must be tracked and kept below 5% of total originated volume.

* **Underwriter Mandate:** May approve overrides up to R 50,000 for Medium Risk applicants failing the PTI rule by no more than 2% (e.g., PTI of 32%).
* **Senior Credit Manager Mandate:** May override a High Risk (Auto-Decline) decision up to a maximum limit of R 25,000 *only if* the applicant is an existing customer whose primary transactional account holds an average daily balance of > R 20,000 over the past 12 months.
* **Credit Committee Mandate:** Any exceptions outside the above parameters require majority approval from the Retail Credit Committee (RCC).
