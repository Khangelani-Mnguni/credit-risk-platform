# Retail Unsecured Lending Credit Policy (South Africa)
Effective Date: January 2026
Applicability: All Retail Unsecured Credit Products (Personal Loans, Credit Cards)

## 1. Regulatory Compliance
All credit assessments must comply strictly with the National Credit Act (NCA) 34 of 2005. 
No credit may be extended without a comprehensive affordability assessment as mandated by the NCA to prevent reckless lending.
Customer data must be processed in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 2. Minimum Qualification Criteria (Knock-out Rules)
An applicant is subject to immediate auto-decline if any of the following are true:
- The applicant is under 18 years of age.
- The applicant does not possess a valid South African Identity Document or permanent residency status.
- The applicant's verifiable gross monthly income is less than R 5,000.
- The applicant is currently under debt review, administration, or has been sequestrated.
- The applicant has a 90+ days in arrears (default) status on any active TransUnion or Experian credit bureau tradeline.

## 3. Affordability and Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limits
The maximum allowable Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio is capped at 45%. 
DTI is calculated as: (Total Monthly Debt Obligations + Proposed Installment) / Gross Monthly Income.
Living expenses must be calculated using the higher of:
a) The customer's declared living expenses.
b) The Minimum Expense Norms table published by the National Credit Regulator (NCR).

## 4. Risk-Based Pricing and Cut-off Strategies
The Probability of Default (PD) generated by the Logistic Regression Scorecard determines the applicant's risk band.

### Risk Band: LOW (PD 0.00% to 10.00%)
- Decision: Auto-Approve.
- Maximum Unsecured Exposure: R 350,000.
- Pricing: Prime + 2% to Prime + 7% depending on the specific PD score.

### Risk Band: MEDIUM (PD 10.01% to 20.00%)
- Decision: Refer to Underwriter / Manual Review.
- Maximum Unsecured Exposure: R 150,000.
- Pricing: Prime + 8% to Prime + 14%.
- Additional Requirement: Requires 3 months of stamped bank statements and manual income verification via employer payslips.

### Risk Band: HIGH (PD > 20.00%)
- Decision: Auto-Decline.
- Exception: Existing customers with a primary transactional account holding > R20,000 average balance for 12 months may be manually overridden by a Senior Credit Manager up to a maximum limit of R 25,000.

## 5. Bureau Inquiries
More than 4 credit inquiries in the last 6 months across the banking sector is considered indicative of credit-seeking behavior. If an applicant has > 4 inquiries, their application must be downgraded by one Risk Band (e.g., Low becomes Medium).
